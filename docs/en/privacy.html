Palang Book Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026 (previous version: July 11, 2026)

In short: Palang Book does not collect personal information.
There are no accounts, no servers of our own, no ads and no analytics tools. Your child’s drawings and voice recordings are stored only on your device. The only thing that ever leaves it is feedback a parent chooses to send with “Send Feedback.”

1. Information we collect

None. Palang Book does not collect, transmit or store any personal information on its own: no names, email addresses, location, usage history or advertising identifiers. The app has no sign-in or registration, and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. For feedback you send yourself, see section 4.

2. Why the app uses device permissions

PermissionPurposeWhere data is kept
CameraTo record a video to trace overApp storage on the device
Photo library (read)To bring in a video to trace overApp storage on the device
Photo library (add)To save a finished animation as a videoYour Photos app
MicrophoneTo record (dub) your child’s voice onto an animationApp storage on the device

Each permission is requested only when you use the related feature, and if you don’t allow it, everything else still works. Content accessed through these permissions is never sent off the device.

3. Internet connection

The app connects to the internet only in these cases: when you open a video from a link, to download that video; when you buy or restore the Lifetime Pass, through Apple’s App Store; and when you send feedback (section 4). Video downloads take place only between your device and the server hosting the video, and no information is passed to the developer or any other third party. Downloaded videos stay on the device and are deleted along with the project. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives your payment details.

4. Sending feedback

If you send feedback with “Send Feedback” on the Support screen, what you wrote (feedback type, message, any screenshots you attached, and the name and email you left for a reply), together with the app version, device model, OS version and language setting, is delivered to the developer through Apple iCloud (CloudKit). It is used only to reply to you and to improve the app, and nothing is sent unless you send it. Name and email are optional. If you’d like feedback you sent to be deleted, let us know at the contact address below. We ask that feedback be sent by a parent or guardian.

5. Children’s privacy

Palang Book is designed to be used by children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, on its own, and it has no ads or social features. The only in-app purchase, the Lifetime Pass, sits behind a grown-up check (a math question) that a child can’t pass alone.

6. Deleting your data

All data lives only on the device, so deleting a project in the app, or deleting the app, removes all related data. There’s no need to ask the developer to delete anything (the one exception is feedback you sent yourself, which we’ll delete on request as described in section 4).

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the update on this page and update the effective date.
